# Privacy Policy – Business Card

Last updated: November 19, 2026

"Business Card" is a student project created at VILNIUS TECH. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

## Data the app stores

- **Profile photo:** If you take a photo with the "Take photo" button, it is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded or shared.
- **CV and portfolio entries:** The entries you add are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded or shared.

All of this data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data with the developer or with third parties. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

## Internet access

The app uses the internet only to download the example images in the portfolio from GitHub. Like any website, GitHub may see technical information such as your IP address when the images are loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

## Contact form

The contact screen does not send anything by itself. It opens your own email app with a prepared message. You decide whether to send it.

## Camera

The camera is only used when you tap "Take photo". The photo stays on your device.
